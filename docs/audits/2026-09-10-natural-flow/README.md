# First-use conversation repair

The owner followed the suggested Samsung FY2023 revenue question with
“2022년과 비교하면 얼마나 증가하거나 감소했어?” and received company clarification.
The bounded follow-up guard rejected the phrasing and removed company context.
The previous maintenance checks did not establish natural first-use usability.

The fix retains company/metric context for bounded financial follow-ups, combines
one requested comparison year with one selected context year, preserves an explicit
pair of requested years, and resolves relative years against a unique selected
fiscal year. Unknown company names and company exclusions still cannot inherit
Samsung evidence. Missing fields are requested individually; missing-period replies
identify available years in the verified snapshot. Comparisons state the calculated
percentage, and the UI explains follow-ups and saved-investigation continuation.

Validation:

- 49 policy tests and 19 PostgreSQL-backed application tests pass.
- Seven real two-turn API flows pass: the owner's wording, short and polite Korean
  comparison variants, relative-year lookup, English comparison, metric switching
  and unsupported-company clarification. Samsung revenue declines 14.33%.
- All 40 existing held-out scenarios pass in one live trial (20/20 per language,
  46 turns) on fb70bd5. All 46 raw model responses and contexts produce identical
  intents and answers when replayed through the final policy after review fixes.
- The real browser repeats the exact first-use sequence, inspects its source,
  saves/reopens/continues the investigation, and passes desktop/mobile reflow and
  axe checks. Test investigations are deleted afterward.
- Frontend build and the architecture/state browser suite pass. Independent Spec
  and Standards review findings about explicit years and duplicated comparison
  vocabulary were fixed and covered by regressions.

The app remains bounded to its verified historical coverage. Relative years with
multiple context years ask for a specific year. Saved answers and public recordings
remain historical records, rather than being regenerated by the new wording.
Continue owner walkthroughs before declaring usability complete.
